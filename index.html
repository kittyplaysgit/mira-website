faq + trust

the fine print, in large type.

does my chat know she's AI?

always. AI disclosure is a hard rule, not a setting — and honestly, it's the bit. your chat knowing she's AI is half the fun.

does mira replace me?

never. she's a sidekick — the co-host who also does the editing. you're the stream.

is she going to spam my chat?

no. she talks when talked to — @mentions, !commands, and alert thank-yous. the only mode where she chats on her own is autopilot, which you flip on deliberately and which always shows its disclosed badge.

which platforms does she work with?

twitch-first today — chat, alerts, VODs, clips. the multi-platform flag merges YouTube, TikTok, and Kick chat into the same brain, and the discord bot brings her to your server between streams.

can she clone my voice or likeness?

no likeness cloning in the box. the "AI You" experiments you may have seen are manual, supervised conversations — not a product feature.

what won't she do?

the persona rails are not configurable: she always discloses she's AI, stays out of politics and religion, gives no medical/legal/financial advice, and won't generate art from IP that isn't yours.

where does my data live?

on your machine. your keys, your database, your clips. nothing routes through us.

do i need a beefy PC?

the VOD clipper wants an NVIDIA GPU for local transcription — the setup wizard detects yours and can disable the clipper if needed. everything else runs light.

what keys do i need?

an Anthropic API key (chat runs on Haiku, VOD analysis on Sonnet, autopilot on Opus) and optionally an ElevenLabs key for voice. the honest-costs table above is what they'll actually cost you.
